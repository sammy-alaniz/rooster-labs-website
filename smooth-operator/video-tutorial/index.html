video tutorial

This video covers how to create a waypoint, a point of interest, how to attach a point of interest to a waypoint, and how to enable the 3D map. POIs allow a DJI drone to 'look' at an object or area as it flys a route that was specified by the user.

Tutorial topics:

  • Add waypoint
  • Move waypoint
  • Attach POI
  • Enable 3D map

DJI Airspaces, ADSB Airplanes, Satellite Imagery

This video shows a user how to enable different map data layers in Smooth Operator Mac OS. Those include DJI Airspaces, which show different restrictions that DJI places on drones during flight. ADSB Airplanes, which shows local aircraft traffic. And Satellite Imagery, to gain a more intuitive insight of where you'll be flying.

This app is for Mac OS and iOS. And is compatible with DJI drones that use the DJI Fly app.
